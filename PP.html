Privacy Policy - Kill The Wave

Developer: KTW Studio  |  Contact: itsfaiz80@gmail.com  |  Last updated: 21 October 2026

Kill The Wave ("the game") is a mobile game published by KTW Studio. This policy explains what information is processed when you play.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information. Game progress (credits, unlocked drones, mission stars and settings) is saved only on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app interactions and approximate location derived from the IP address. Google uses this data according to its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or limit ad personalisation at any time in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. Rewarded ads

Watching a rewarded ad is always optional. The reward (in-game credits) is stored only on your device.

4. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

5. Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device. Advertising data held by Google can be managed through your Google account and device settings.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
